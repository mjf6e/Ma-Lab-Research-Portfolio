The lab team

Current members, research staff, and trainees of the Ma Laboratory at UVA. New students: this is who you'll be working alongside, and roughly what each person focuses on. Sourced from the DOSS Lab Directory ↗ and the PI's CV.

Research faculty & scientists

Who's working on what

Jongsoo Kim Research Asst. Professor, 2025

MG29 (SYPL2) and MG53 (TRIM72) in skeletal muscle aging, metabolic regulation, and cardioprotection, combining molecular biology, mouse models, contractility analysis, and AAV gene delivery to find therapeutic targets for sarcopenia and age-associated cardiac dysfunction.

Kyung Eun Lee

Muscle physiology in muscular dystrophy and age-related sarcopenia, with a growing interest in translating those mechanistic insights to neurodegenerative disease.

Yuchen Chen Research Asst. Professor, 2025

Skeletal muscle–organ communication and immune/oxidative-homeostasis mechanisms in sepsis, aging, autoimmune disease, and lung cancer; translational development of protein biologics, antibodies, and peptide therapeutics (mouse models, in vitro citrullination assays).

Chuanxi Cai

Molecular and cellular therapeutic strategies for tissue injury and repair, with a focus on heart and lung pathology, plus cancer research on age-associated tumorigenesis and targets that inhibit it.

Jae-Kyun (James) Ko, PhD

Probiotics-released extracellular vesicles, muscle autophagy, and gene regulation of MG53 and MG29, using CRISPR/Cas9 editing, shRNA/RNAi silencing, and site-directed mutagenesis.

Xiaoliang Wang

Cardiac physiology, mechanisms of myocardial injury and therapeutic strategies.

Xinyu Zhou Imaging core

Cardiac and skeletal muscle biology and disease, calcium signaling in muscle physiology and pathology, and imaging-method development. Runs the lab's advanced microscopy: a Zeiss Stellaris 5 confocal with live-cell imaging, a Zeiss ratiometric Ca²⁺ imaging microscope, a Zeiss pathology slide scanner, and a quantitative image-analysis workstation.

Ki Ho Park

Contributes across the lab's MG53 cardioprotection and corneal-repair work (see Publications).
